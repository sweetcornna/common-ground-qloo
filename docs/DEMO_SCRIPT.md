# Optional demonstration script

Target: approximately three minutes. The working application is the primary deliverable; a recording is optional. Never edit a demo-mode banner away or present fixtures as authenticated Qloo results.

| Time | Screen action | Suggested narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Show title and mode banner. | “Common Ground helps two people discover something together without letting one person's taste dominate. This run is [live Qloo / a clearly labelled offline demonstration].” |
| 0:20–0:50 | Select movie, artist and book seeds for both people. | “Each person brings their own cultural starting points. The live application resolves these to Qloo entities.” |
| 0:50–1:15 | Choose 60 minutes and balanced focus; generate. | “The workflow gathers candidates for both profiles, compares their returned ranks and applies the session constraints.” |
| 1:15–1:50 | Open recommendation evidence, alternatives and trace. | “These are returned-list positions, not probabilities. The JSON export also contains our computed policy scores. The trace shows the work performed. Our prompts suggest how to sample or discuss each work; we do not provide playback or availability.” |
| 1:50–2:20 | Reject a recommendation and rebuild. | “That rejection becomes a hard exclusion. The workflow runs again and replaces the suggestion.” |
| 2:20–2:40 | Compare adventurous focus if candidates permit. | “A different policy changes how we balance shared familiarity and exploration. The underlying evidence remains visible.” |
| 2:40–3:00 | Show source/readme and disclosure. | “LangGraph coordinates the process, Qloo grounds live retrieval, and the project documents AI assistance and its current validation status.” |

Before recording, use only preferences participants consent to show; hide terminals and secret settings. In offline mode say “fixture recommendation” when discussing candidates. After live validation, use actual successful searches and describe the trace shown on screen; do not promise an exact recommendation or imply results were hand-selected by Qloo for a scripted story.

Suggested screenshots: two participant profiles; the three-act plan with visible mode; a candidate's rank evidence; the feedback-driven revised plan. Keep the mode and limitations legible.
